🧠 Hybrid Ensemble Architecture

EmoSense AI blends deep contextual transformers (RoBERTa) with lexical feature engineering (XGBoost + NRC Lexicon) via dynamic alpha-weighted soft voting:

Ensemble Formula: Pensemble = α · PRoBERTa + (1 − α) · PXGBoost
